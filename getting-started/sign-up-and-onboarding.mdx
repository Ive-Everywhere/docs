Eluu's onboarding has three screens. By the time you finish, you have a team, a default colleague, a demo file, a demo view, and an active trial.

## Step 1 — Sign up

Open [app.eluu.ai](https://app.eluu.ai) and pick a sign-in method:

- **Google** — fastest. Continues with the Google profile you pick.
- **Microsoft** — same idea, for Microsoft accounts.
- **Email and password** — standard flow.

If you've been invited to a team via email, click the link in the invitation. We'll handle that flow below.

## Step 2 — Onboarding

After your first sign-in, you'll land in the onboarding flow. It's three screens, none of them long.

### Screen 1 — Profile

Tell Eluu about you.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | Pre-filled from your sign-in |
| Job title | Yes | What you do |
| Company website | Yes | Helps Eluu personalise your default colleague |
| LinkedIn URL | No | Validated as a URL if you fill it in |

Click **Continue**. There's no Back button — once you've answered, you move on.

### Screen 2 — Invite decision (only if you've been invited)

If you have a pending invitation to someone else's team, you'll see a card with the team's name, the inviter's name, and the role they've assigned you. Two buttons:

- **Accept & Join** — drops you into the team. Onboarding ends here.
- **Skip** — continues to the carousel and creates a brand-new team for you.

If you haven't been invited, this screen doesn't show.

### Screen 3 — Meet Eluu carousel

A short three-slide tour of Sessions, Views, and Integrations.

While you watch, Eluu provisions the rest of your account in the background:

- Creates your team (if you didn't accept an invite).
- Builds your default colleague.
- Drops a demo file in your Hard Drive.
- Builds a demo view.
- Activates your trial credits.
- Starts your first session.

This takes 10-15 seconds. The bottom of the screen shows progress — `Colleague is being brewed`. When it's done, the **Get started** button appears. Click it.

If something goes wrong, you'll see an error message and a **Retry** button. Clicking Retry re-runs the provisioning.

## Step 3 — You land in your first chat

After **Get started**, you arrive in a chat with your default colleague. From here, [Your first 5 minutes](/getting-started/your-first-5-minutes) takes you through the rest.

## What if I close the tab mid-onboarding?

Closing the tab before pressing **Get started** is fine. When you log back in, you'll resume where you stopped. Eluu remembers what you completed and what's still pending.

If you've completed onboarding once, the flow won't trigger again. The sidebar will show a "Complete onboarding" prompt if you backed out before the carousel finished.

## Invitation flows in detail

There are a few variants depending on how you arrive at Eluu.

### You're brand new and have no invitation

Standard three-screen onboarding above.

### You're brand new and clicked an invite link

The invite link goes to `/invite/{token}`. Eluu validates the invitation (rejecting expired or wrong-account invitations) and routes you to onboarding with the invite attached. After your profile screen you'll skip directly into the invited team.

### You already have an Eluu account and clicked an invite link

You'll see an invite-acceptance modal — no full onboarding flow, since you're already set up.

- **Accept** — joins the team. You're switched to it.
- **Decline** — dismisses the invite.
- **Wrong account?** — the modal offers a way to switch accounts or go to your dashboard.

### You already have an Eluu account but the invite was sent to a different email

You'll see a "Switch account" or "Go to dashboard" prompt — the accept and decline buttons don't show until you sign in to the right account.

### You already have an Eluu account and there's a pending invite from a teammate

The next time you open the app, the sidebar shows up to two invitation cards. Click **View** to open the acceptance modal, or **Dismiss** to decline silently.

## What gets created

A single backend call wires up everything when you finish the carousel. It:

1. Updates your profile with the form data.
2. Creates a new team (unless you joined an existing one).
3. Creates your default colleague.
4. Drops a demo file in the Hard Drive.
5. Builds a demo view.
6. Activates your trial credits.
7. Starts your first session.

If any of these fail, the system retries. If retries fail, you'll see an error toast and a path to support.
