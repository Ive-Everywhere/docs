Eluu is the platform businesses use to run operations with AI. You don't buy an "AI tool". You build **colleagues** — focused AI teammates that own a business function and execute work across every tool you've connected.

Each colleague is the equivalent of an AI SaaS product you'd otherwise buy and stitch together. A sales colleague does what a Decimal AI or Clay does. A customer support colleague does what an Intercom Fin or Decagon does. A meeting-intelligence colleague does what a Granola or Otter does. Except: it's one platform, one bill, one shared memory, and one set of tools.

## The core idea

Most AI SaaS products own one workflow. Sales has its own. Customer support has its own. Research has its own. Each one is per-seat, each one has its own admin panel, and each one has zero shared context with the others.

Eluu inverts that. The unit of value is the **colleague** — and the colleague is what scales, not the seat count.

| Org size | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Small / personal | One colleague covers a whole function. A single "sales colleague" does prospecting, outreach, and pipeline tracking. |
| Startup | A few colleagues, each owning one function. Sales, customer success, ops, marketing. |
| Scaling | Many colleagues, each more focused. One colleague for meeting intelligence, one for renewals, one for inbound triage. |
| Enterprise | Dozens of colleagues running real workloads across the org, in parallel, on schedules, with verification loops. |

You add colleagues as you grow. You don't add tools.

## What you actually get

Each colleague has:

- **A name and a role** — "Sofia, our senior sales operations colleague".
- **A persistent memory** — a Git-backed workspace that accumulates context across every session forever.
- **A set of abilities** — the tools you've connected, plus packaged skills, plus custom playbooks.
- **A surface** — reachable from the web app, from Slack mentions, from the browser extension.

You can build as many colleagues as your plan allows, each one focused on a different role.

## The two-stage path to value

Most teams start with the first stage and grow into the second.

### Stage 1 — A multi-modal business harness
You use Eluu the way you'd use a sharp new teammate. Research, light automation, organising documents, drafting and triage. The colleague reads from your tools and writes back. Useful from day one.

### Stage 2 — Automated workflows that move the business
Once colleagues are working, you turn them into [scheduled jobs](/jobs/overview). The morning briefing runs at 8am every weekday. The pipeline review runs every Friday. The customer health check runs every two hours. With verification loops, the colleague catches its own mistakes before you see them. This is where the operational lift gets real.

## Who Eluu is for

Eluu is built for **operators** — the people running business functions. Sales leaders, customer success heads, ops managers, marketing leaders, founders. The people who already own outcomes and wish their tools moved faster.

If you've ever stitched together three AI SaaS subscriptions, a Zapier zap, and a spreadsheet to get one workflow running, Eluu is for you.

## What makes it different

- **One platform, one bill.** You stop paying per-seat for every AI SaaS in your stack.
- **Persistent learning memory.** Every interaction adds context. Over weeks, your colleague becomes harder to replace, not easier.
- **Cross-tool execution.** Real action across Gmail, Slack, Sheets, Salesforce, Linear, and more — not only summaries.
- **Slack-native.** Mention a colleague directly in any channel. Multi-bot architecture means each colleague can have its own brand.
- **Multi-modal harness.** Web app + Slack + browser extension, one coherent surface. No switching between AI products.
- **No-code.** Build, configure, and use without writing a line of code.
